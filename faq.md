# Powerlifter: questions and answers

Powerlifter is a training log for conjugate and Westside-style powerlifting on iPhone, with an Apple Watch companion. This page answers the questions people ask most.

---

## Getting started

### What do I need to run Powerlifter?

An iPhone on iOS 26 or later. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 26 or later on an Apple Watch Series 6, SE (2nd generation), Ultra or newer, and Powerlifter Plus.

### Is there an iPad, Android or Mac version?

There's no iPad, Android or Mac version yet. The first release is for iPhone only; iPad and Android are planned for later.

### I can't find Powerlifter in the App Store in my country.

Powerlifter isn't sold in the European Union for now. It is offered in every other App Store region. Search for "conjugate" or use a direct link, since another app has a similar name.

### What happens the first time I open the app?

You choose how you'll use it: **Timer only** or **Workout logging**. If you pick Workout logging, two short pages follow:

1. **Setup:** your body weight (kg or lb), and whether Powerlifter may read Apple Health and your calendars. Each one has **Not now**, and saying no never blocks the rest.
2. **Bars:** tick the bars your gym has. The straight bar is always on. Powerlifter then makes ready-to-use setups for the main lifts on those bars.

**Skip and use every bar** adds every bar and skips the questions.

### What is Timer only?

Timer only gives you the interval timer without the workout log. You can switch to Workout logging later in **Data controls → Mode**, and the bar checklist runs once at that point.

### Where are the settings?

Tap the **KG** or **LB** button at the top left of Home, Progress or Library. It has the unit switch, **Appearance**, **Data controls…** and **Help**. In Timer only there is no KG / LB button (no weights): tap the gear at the top left instead. It opens the same menu with **Appearance**, **Data controls…** and **Help**, and no unit switch.

### Where is help?

Tap the **KG** or **LB** button at the top left of Home, Progress or Library, then **Help**. It opens this list, with a search field at the top. In Timer only, tap the gear at the top left, then **Help**.

### Can I ask Siri how to do something in Powerlifter?

Yes. Say **"Ask Powerlifter how do I log bar speed"**, or swap in what you want to do. Siri reads a short answer and shows a card with the steps and an **Open in Powerlifter** button that takes you to the full answer here.

You can also say **"Ask Powerlifter a question"**. Siri asks what you want to know, then looks it up. If there's no answer for it, Siri says so and Help opens with your words in the search field.

Siri only reads Powerlifter's own written answers. Nothing is made up. The answers are built into the app, and nothing is sent to us. It can't answer questions about your own training numbers.

### How do I switch between kilograms and pounds?

Tap the **KG** / **LB** button at the top left and choose **Change to pounds** or **Change to kilograms**. Everything is stored exactly, so switching never changes your numbers. A new install starts in pounds in the US and kilograms everywhere else.

### Can one lift use kilograms while the rest of the app uses pounds?

Yes, in two ways:

- **Every time you do an exercise:** in **Library**, open the exercise and set **Default unit** to **Kilograms (kg)** or **Pounds (lbs)**. New lifts of that exercise start in that unit. **App default** follows the KG / LB button.
- **Just today's lift:** in a workout, long-press the lift's title and tap **Use kilograms** or **Use pounds**. Only that lift changes.

Handy for kettlebells or a competition bar loaded in kilos while your gym's plates are in pounds.

## Logging a workout

### How do I start a workout?

Tap **+** on Home. The logger opens on a new workout dated now. Tap **Add exercise** to pick a lift. To log a past day, select that day on the calendar first, then tap **+**.

If your coach writes the plan on paper or a whiteboard, tap the **camera** on Home instead and take a photo of it. Powerlifter reads the sheet and fills in the workout for you to check (see the question about starting a workout from a photo).

### How do I log a set?

Type the weight and reps, then tap the check to mark it **Made**, or mark it **Miss** with a reason if you want one. Planned sets are checked off one at a time, top to bottom. Made, Miss and delete each offer **Undo** for a few seconds.

### What weight do I type: the bar and plates, or the total with bands?

Type the **bar plus plates**: the free weight. Bands and chains are part of the setup, so Powerlifter adds them for you where they matter. Your 1RMs and records always count the free weight only.

### What does the plate loader show?

The plates that go on each side of the bar for the weight you're about to lift, drawn on the bar you picked. It is display only.

### How do I record bar speed?

Bar speed is free and works with or without a meter.

- **For one lift today:** long-press the lift's title and tap **Log bar speed today**. Each set gets an **m/s** field. Type the speed from your meter or your coach. Only that lift changes.
- **Every time you use a setup:** in **Library**, open the setup and set **Also log** to bar speed. Each new lift on that setup then has the m/s field.
- **With a supported velocity meter:** turn the meter on, then tap the meter icon at the top of the workout (or **Add a bar-speed meter** on a set) and tap **Connect**. If a free meter is nearby, Powerlifter offers it once by itself. Rep speeds then fill in as you lift, and after the lift you can check them in **Bar speed**: whose reps they were, and drop a bad reading.
- **On Apple Watch** (Plus): turn the crown to set the m/s on the set.

The meter's own app and Powerlifter can't both be connected at once, so close the maker's app first. Logging never waits on a meter: if it won't connect, just type the speed.

### How do I log a bodyweight lift like pull-ups?

Log only the weight you add (or take off with assistance). The lift shows it as **BW+20**, for example. Your body weight comes from the number you saved in **Data controls → Body weight & Health**.

### How do I finish a workout?

Tap **Finish** in the logger. If you close the logger instead, the workout stays in progress and Home shows a card with **Open** and **Finish**. A workout that's under 15 minutes asks whether to save or discard it.

### How do I find an old workout?

Home is the calendar plus your history. Scroll down or search it by lift name or date. While you type, the calendar steps aside so the results sit above the keyboard, and the search looks at every day, whichever day is selected. Clear the search and the calendar comes back where you left it.

### How do I see what I did last time on this lift?

Tap **Previous lifts** on the lift (the clock-and-arrow button in its header, or **Prev** in the Dock layout). You can filter to **All**, **Work** or **1RM**, and copy a past day into this workout.

### Can I start a workout from a photo of my written plan?

Yes. Tap the camera on Home and take or pick a photo of the sheet. Text recognition runs on your iPhone; the picture is never sent to us. You review every line before it becomes a workout. This is a beta feature.

### How do I log an injury?

Hold **+** on Home, or hold a date on the calendar, and choose **Injury**. Type what hurts (for example "Left shoulder"), pick the day it started, and save. Earlier injuries show as one-tap name chips so you don't retype them. It's a note in your journal: it doesn't change your workouts or hide any exercise.

You don't have to say when an injury is over. **Still going** stays on until you end it, and nothing nags you. When you're ready, hold a date (or **+**) inside the injury and choose **End** with its name, or tap the injury's row and turn **Still going** off. Turning it back on reopens it if it flares up. In Year in Review, the Training card and Side by side show how many injuries touched the year and how many days you were injured (counted only for injuries you ended), and the Journal lists each one. Injuries are for your own record, not medical advice.

## Exercises, setups and groups

### What's the difference between an exercise and a setup?

- An **exercise** is what the lift *is*: Box squat, 2" deficit box squat, Floor press, Sumo deadlift. A height, pin, board count or stance makes a different exercise, because you'd look up its max separately.
- A **setup** is how the exercise is loaded that day: which bar, which bands or chains, and any extra load. "Box squat · Safety squat bar · #2 bands" is a setup.

You log a setup. Progress can show your numbers by exercise or by the exact setup.

### What are groups?

Groups are folders for browsing the library and filtering Progress, like "Squat" or "Bench". An exercise can be in more than one group. Groups don't change how anything is logged.

### How do I add bands or chains to a lift?

Make a setup with them: **Library → Setups → +**, pick the exercise and bar, then add the bands or chains. You can also copy an existing setup (swipe **Copy**) and change only the bands. During a workout, long-press the lift's title and tap **Change bands / chains…**.

### I added a bar to my gym. Where are its setups?

Adding a bar in **Library → Gym → Bars** creates setups for the main lifts that bar is used for. Other lifts get a setup the first time you log them on it.

### Why can't I delete an exercise, bar or band?

Anything a logged workout uses can't be deleted, so your history never loses its parts. You can rename it. One you've never logged can be deleted: in **Library**, swipe it left and tap **Delete**.

### I changed a bar's weight (or a setup's bands). Does my old history change?

To change a bar, tap it in **Library → Gym → Bars**. Powerlifter asks first: when an edit would change numbers you've already logged, Save tells you how many lifts it touches and offers the safe choice first.

## Bands and chains

### How does Powerlifter know how much my bands add?

You tell it, once. Bands pull differently on each lift, so each pair of bands has a **lockout** number for each lift (squat, bench, deadlift, rack pull, shoulder press). Enter them in **Library → Gym → Paired bands → Lockouts…**. Chains are entered by their weight.

Powerlifter never guesses. If a lockout is blank, the setup shows **Not set** and nothing is added.

### How do I measure my band tension?

Bands pull differently for every setup, so measure yours at lockout and at the bottom, with a scale or a load cell, and enter what you get. Powerlifter never guesses.

### What's the "bottom" number for bands?

The band tension at the bottom of the lift. It counts toward that day's volume. Set it with the **Top | Bottom** switch on the Lockouts page. A blank bottom counts as zero.

### My bands are set up differently at another rack. How do I record that?

Use an **anchor**: a second column of lockout numbers for the same bands on the same lift, for example "Spotter arms" or a specific rack. A new lift starts on the anchor you used last time on that setup.

### Do bands count in my 1RM?

No. Your 1RMs and records are the free weight on the bar. On **Progress → 1RM**, a chip lets the chart blend in a share of the band and chain tension; it starts at 50%.

### Can I see my band tension as a percent of my max?

Yes. When you add bands or chains to a setup, the screen shows the lockout total and what percent of that lift's 1RM it is (for example "70 lb · 14%"). The 1RM is your heaviest made single on that exercise in the last 12 months, bar and plates only.

## Max-effort days and 1RMs

### How do I build a max-effort ladder?

In a workout, tap **1RM** on the lift. Enter the target and Powerlifter plans the warm-ups and singles from the empty bar up (a deadlift starts with plates on the bar), with even jumps and the last two about 5% apart. You can edit any row before you apply it. Turn on **Lazy plating** on that screen to avoid plates under 5 lb (2.5 kg) a side where the rules allow. It's free.

### What are the suggested numbers in the 1RM planner?

Estimates from your own past max-effort days: first the same exercise on other setups, then related lifts. A partial lift (like a rack pull) never feeds a full one. They're suggestions; you pick the target.

### Where do I see my max-effort history?

**Progress → 1RM** lists every max-effort day as a ladder, with a chart. A day counts when it has five or more singles that climb. Rows with other than seven singles show **short** or **over**. You can star a plan that worked.

### How do I load 40% of my 1RM quickly?

On the lift's next empty work set, tap **Fill** (it reads **% 1RM** when the lift has no past loads) and pick a percent from 25 to 90, in 5% steps. It fills that set with that share of your max, rounded to your plates, and shows a line like "40% of 405 lb · Box squat" so you can see where it came from. To plan a whole template at one percent, like 8×8, use the **% 1RM** chip beside **Weight** on the **Set template** sheet. That is also the way in the Dock layout.

The max is your heaviest made single on that exercise in the last 12 months, bar and plates only. If the exercise has none, it uses the main competition lift (squat, bench or deadlift) and names it. The percents show only on barbell lifts that have a max to use. Bands and chains aren't counted.

### Where are my all-time records and my total?

**Progress → Benchmarks.** The **Total** at the top shows your best squat, bench and deadlift and your total, with Gym and Meet side by side. The chip beside the heading ranks both columns by **Load**, **Wilks**, **DOTS** or **Relative strength** (load ÷ body weight). Wilks and DOTS need your sex from Apple Health; Relative strength doesn't. These are always your all-time bests; the range buttons change only the rows below. Gym and Meet show the same details: **Load** shows the heaviest and its date, and the others add the score and your weight that day. That weight comes from the workout (a meet's weigh-in), else Apple Health that day, else your nearest weight in the 30 days before. With none near it, tap **Add weight** on a score view to save one with that workout; it doesn't go to Apple Health, and it shows on that day on Home. When a lift with no weight could beat your best, **N could beat this** lists them, so you can add only the weights that matter. Powerlifter never guesses a weight. Below it are the lifts you've chosen to track and your other tests (max reps, max hold, time).

### Why didn't my heavy single show as a PR?

A PR is the most free weight you've ever made on that setup or exercise. Bands, chains and extra load don't count, and the first time you log a lift there's nothing to beat yet. PRs are judged when you tap Made during a workout in progress. You can turn the celebration off in **Appearance → Logger → Celebrate PRs**.

## Timers

### How do I start a timer?

Tap the timer on Home's toolbar. Pick a face (**Clock**, **W/R** for work and rest, **Hold**, or **Marks**), set it, and start. To keep a timer with a lift, tap **Timer** on that lift, set it, and tap **Save to lift**; after that the lift's **▶** starts it.

### Does the timer check off my sets?

The Clock and W/R faces check off a planned set each interval when they're attached to a lift. Hold logs each finished hold on a timed lift. Marks never touch your sets. The timer on Home checks nothing off.

### Does the timer keep going when my phone is locked?

Yes. It runs on the clock, so it stays right if you lock the phone or switch apps, and it carries on even if iOS closes the app. On a locked phone you get a short notification at each change, unless an Apple Watch with Plus is buzzing for you.

### Why is the timer silent?

Sound is off until you turn it on. On the timer's setup page, open **Haptics**, make sure **Haptics & cues** is on, then turn on **Sound with cues**. Beeps and voice follow your iPhone's silent switch unless you also turn on **Play on silent**. The Apple Watch only buzzes.

### Can I control the timer with Siri?

Yes: start, pause, resume, skip, stop, and how much time is left.

### What's on my lock screen during a workout?

A Live Activity on the lock screen and Dynamic Island shows the next set, its plates, and the running timer. It's display only and free.

## Powerlifter Plus

### Why should I buy Plus?

Plus unlocks the extras listed below, and it's also how you support the app. Powerlifter is built independently by a lifter who uses it every training day. It took a great deal of work to make, and it has no ads, sells no data, and has no investors behind it. If it helps your training, Plus is what keeps it going and getting better.

### Do I have to pay to log workouts?

No. Logging, Progress (Sessions, 1RM, Benchmarks), Review, the 1RM planner, plan from photo, velocity meters and the Live Activity need no purchase. Plus is an optional unlock.

### What does Plus unlock?

- The Apple Watch app
- The **Iron** and **Clipboard** looks
- The **Dock** logger layout
- The **Scoreboard** and **Pad** timer faces
- The other Home Screen icons

### How much is Plus, and is there a free trial?

- **Monthly:** $1.99 a month, with 6 months free
- **Yearly:** $9.99 a year, with 6 months free
- **Lifetime:** $29.99 once, no trial

These are US prices. The app shows the price for your region. The free trial is once per Apple Account.

### How do I buy or restore Plus?

**Data controls → Plus**. To get Plus back on a new phone or after reinstalling, tap **Restore purchases** there while signed into the same Apple Account.

### Can my family use my Plus?

Yes. Family Sharing is on for all three plans.

### How do I cancel?

Apple handles subscriptions. Cancel in **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** on your iPhone, or from **Data controls → Plus → Manage subscription** while a subscription is active. A subscription renews until you cancel it.

### I bought Lifetime but I still have a subscription.

Buying Lifetime doesn't cancel a subscription; only you can, through Apple. Right after the purchase Powerlifter opens Apple's subscription page so you can cancel it. Plus stays on either way.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use **reportaproblem.apple.com**, or the Report a Problem link on your App Store receipt.

### What happens if my Plus ends?

Nothing you logged is lost. The app goes back to the Standard look, the Classic logger, the Ring timer and the Bend icon, and remembers your choices. When Plus comes back, so do they.

## Apple Watch

### Does the Watch app work on its own?

No. It's a companion. Start the workout on your iPhone; then the Watch can run the floor: Made, Miss with a reason, rounds, and the timer. Your phone can stay in your bag. The Watch never changes weights or adds notes.

### The Watch app just says to open Powerlifter on my iPhone.

That's expected when no workout or timer is running. It wakes up when you start today's workout or a timer on the phone. With **Open automatically** on (the default), it opens by itself.

### How do I install the Watch app?

In the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to **Available Apps** and tap **Install** next to Powerlifter. You need Plus to use it.

### Can the Watch save my workout to Fitness and Health?

Yes, if you turn on **Record in Fitness**. Your Watch then saves a Strength workout with heart rate and active energy, and asks how hard it felt (Easy to All Out) when you finish. It's off until you choose it. Turn it on or off in **Data controls → Apple Watch**.

## Apple Health

### What does Powerlifter read from Apple Health?

Only with your permission:

- **Body mass**, to suggest your body weight, to show a day's weight on Home and to chart your history on Progress. A suggested weight is saved only when you confirm it. The weights shown for past days and on the chart are read from Health each time, and Powerlifter doesn't keep them.
- **Biological sex**, for DOTS and Wilks scores.
- With **Record in Fitness** on, your Watch reads your heart rate during the workout to show it on the wrist.

### What does Powerlifter write to Apple Health?

Two things, and only if you allow them:

- **A body weight you type.** A weigh-in, **Body weight & Health** or a meet weigh-in saves it to Health for that day. Save again that day and yours is replaced. **Remove this weigh-in** deletes it. A number that came from Health is never written back, and weights from your scale or other apps are never changed.
- **Workouts.** With Record in Fitness on, a Strength workout with heart rate, active energy and your effort rating.

### I said no to Health. How do I turn it on?

In the Health app, go to **Sharing → Apps → Powerlifter** and turn on what you want. Then use **Read Apple Health** in **Data controls → Body weight & Health**.

### Does Powerlifter keep a body-weight history?

Apple Health keeps it. Powerlifter stores your current body weight and any weight you save on a workout (a meet weigh-in, or one you add to a lift on Progress), and reads the rest from Health. Tap a day on Home and that day's weight shows under the date, whether you typed it or a scale sent it to Health. Tap the weight to open that day's weigh-in. A new weight replaces that day's once Health lets Powerlifter save it. Without that, a day shows the weight saved with that day's workout, and a scale's weight stays as it is.

For the whole history, open Progress, choose Benchmarks, and tap Body weight for a line chart over the range you pick. Touch and hold the chart, then slide, to read a day.

Without Health access, you see only a weight saved on that day's workout. The Body weight row asks you to connect Apple Health until Powerlifter has asked once. After that, Health doesn't tell an app it said no, so a refusal looks the same as having no weights: the row reads No weights yet, and its page explains and offers Open Health.

## iCloud, backups and your data

### Is my training backed up?

If your iPhone is signed into iCloud, Apple keeps a copy in your own private iCloud, automatically and at no cost. A new phone or a reinstall brings it back. We can't read it. **Data controls** shows whether iCloud is On, Offline, or needs you to sign in.

### Does it sync between my devices?

Your training log syncs through iCloud to any iPhone signed into the same Apple Account. Settings like your unit, look and timer sounds stay on each phone.

### How do I know sync is working?

Open **Data controls**. Under **iCloud** it says **Last synced** with a time or a date.

- **Not synced yet** means this install hasn't finished a sync.
- **Couldn't sync. It will try again.** usually just means no connection right now. There's nothing to do.

After a reinstall or on a new phone, your history can take a while to come down, and it arrives in batches. Until then, Home can show an empty log with **Getting your workouts from iCloud…**, and your library can show the same bar twice. Your workouts are safe in iCloud. Leave the doubles alone: they merge on their own once iCloud catches up.

### What if my iCloud storage is full?

Your log stays safe on your iPhone and you can keep training. It just stops syncing until there's room. Free up space or add storage in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**, and syncing picks up where it left off. Until then, an **Export backup** now and then is a good idea.

### I see the same workout twice.

That usually happens when a backup is imported on a phone that also syncs through iCloud, so the history arrives twice. Delete the extra copy and the deletion syncs to your other devices. To avoid it, import a backup on one phone only, and let iCloud bring it to the rest.

### Can I export my data?

Yes. **Data controls → Data**:

- **Export backup…** saves a full backup you can import again.
- **Export history spreadsheet…** saves a CSV with one row per set.

The file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

### How do I restore a backup?

**Data controls → Data → Import history…** and pick a Powerlifter backup file. A day that already has a workout is skipped, so importing twice doesn't duplicate anything.

### How do I delete my data?

In **Data controls → Data**:

- **Delete all session data…** removes every workout, set, max-effort plan and sheet photo, on this phone and on your other devices through iCloud. Your library (exercises, bars, gym) stays.
- **Factory reset…** also clears your library and puts the defaults back. Your Plus purchase and body weight are kept.

Both untick your treatment calendars on this phone, so it won't bring those days back. Another device with them still ticked can.

Neither can be undone, so export a backup first if you might want it. To remove the iCloud copy itself: **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Powerlifter**.

### Do I need an account?

No. There's no Powerlifter account and no Powerlifter server. Your Apple Account handles iCloud and purchases.

## Privacy

### What data do you collect?

None. The App Store label is **Data Not Collected**. There are no ads, analytics or tracking. Your log stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

### Why does Powerlifter ask for my calendars, camera or Bluetooth?

Each one is optional and only for one feature:

- **Calendars:** to mark treatment days (physio, massage) from calendars you pick. It reads the date and title and never changes events.
- **Camera and photos:** for plan from photo. Recognition runs on your iPhone.
- **Bluetooth:** to read rep speeds from a supported velocity meter during a workout. Only after you tap the meter icon.

### Is Powerlifter medical or coaching advice?

No. It's a log, not a coach or a medical device. You're responsible for your own programming and safety. For training or injury questions, ask your coach or a doctor.
