# Privacy Policy — Powerlifter

**Effective date:** 2026-09-27
**App:** Powerlifter for iPhone, with its Apple Watch companion
**Contact:** powerlifterapp@icloud.com


---

## The short version

Powerlifter is a training log. What you enter stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. If your iPhone is signed into iCloud, Apple keeps a copy in **your own private iCloud database** so a new phone or a reinstall can bring it back. There is no Powerlifter account and no Powerlifter server. We don't collect, see, sell or share your data, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

---

## What the app stores, and where

### Training data

This covers workouts, sets, weights, exercises and setups, timers, notes, max-effort plans, gym inventory, treatment / vacation / sick days, year notes, and your settings.

- **On your devices.** Stored on your iPhone. The Apple Watch app gets what it needs to show the current workout from your iPhone.
- **In your iCloud.** When the iPhone is signed into iCloud, Apple syncs this data to your **private** CloudKit database (`iCloud.com.mile.Powerlifter`). Only your Apple Account can read it. We can't.
- **Exports.** A backup or spreadsheet exists only if you export it, and it goes only where you choose to save or share it.

### Photos (plan from photo)

When you use plan from photo, the app reads the picture you take or pick **on your iPhone**, using Apple's on-device text recognition. The picture is not uploaded anywhere. If you save the sheet with a workout, it is stored with that workout, which means in your private iCloud as well when sync is on.

### Apple Health

With your permission, Powerlifter:

- reads **body mass**, for bodyweight lifts such as pull-ups;
- reads **biological sex**, for Wilks and DOTS scores;
- uses **workout** access so the iPhone can open the Watch app;
- with **Record in Fitness** turned on, saves a Strength workout and **heart rate** from your Apple Watch to Health.

Health data is never used for advertising and is never sent to us. You can change access in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Powerlifter.

### Calendar (treatment calendars)

If you turn on treatment calendars, Powerlifter reads the **date and title** of events in the calendars you pick, so it can mark those days. It never adds, changes or deletes events. You can turn access off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

### Bluetooth (velocity meters)

If you connect a velocity meter, Powerlifter talks to it over Bluetooth during an open workout and stores the rep speeds with that lift. Nothing about nearby devices is kept or sent anywhere.

### Purchases (Powerlifter Plus)

Apple handles every purchase. The app only learns whether your Apple Account has Plus so it can unlock those features. We never see your payment details.

---

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and no server of its own.

If you have agreed to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports, under Apple's privacy policy. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

---

## Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until you delete it. In the app, Data controls can delete your sessions, or reset everything. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. The iCloud copy stays in your private iCloud until you delete it, which you can do from your iPhone in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Powerlifter.
